# Design review, 2026-10-19

Current site (transdimension.uk, Mac display) against the second-pass design (dev server on td/final-design-slots + design/final-port-2, iPad display). Kim narrates, notes taken live. Each page: keep from current, keep from new, undecided.

## Home

- New: logo looked wrong scale and position. Measured at equal width: scale identical (527px at 1500+), the scale difference on the day was the Mac (1512, 527px tier) vs iPad (1366, 381px tier). Position was real: 56px right of live at desktop because the intro panel's 72px padding shifted the absolutely positioned h1. Fixed with `left: 0` on `.td-home__logo`.

- Keep from current: the larger type. Kim prefers live's sizes over the design's even though the new one is the more accurate copy. Measured at 1512: intro 19.2px vs 16px, subtitle 51.2px vs 40px, event card title 24px vs 20px, card details 19.2px vs 16px. News card already matched. Applied live's sizes (intro 1.2rem, subtitle back to 3.2rem, card title 1.5rem, details 1.2rem).
- Keep from current: the intro panel spacing. Pass two had replaced the Elm values with the design's (64/72/56px padding, 600px measure, 24px gaps); live has 48/16px padding, 2rem 8rem paragraph margins, 32px above and 80px below the paragraph. Restored the Elm values. Kim: "how are we so off sync from whatever the elm source says?" Answer: pass two followed Home.jsx, not Elm, by design; the preference is now Elm spacing, design visuals.
- Keep from current: the copy. Restored "See what's on", "Upcoming Events", "View all events", "Latest news" (pass two had the handoff doc's "See what's on near you", "Featured events", "See more events", "Latest update").
- Event cards: keep dev's wider left/right padding inside the events box, otherwise match live's card. Applied live geometry: 56px date column with 1rem gap (was 70/10), day 3.12rem (was 2.6), month 1.2rem/1.5 (was 0.85/1.25), title line-height 1.25 (was 1.3), 8px between the rule and the first detail (was 0). Colours were already live's defaults (rule pink, day white, month pink); the screenshot Kim pasted was the hover state. Top of the day number now sits level with the title top, as on live.
- Core fix (PlaceCal td/final-design-slots): Bullet N+1 on event cards, `EventsQuery#base_scope` now preloads `online_address` and `address: :neighbourhood`. Also merged main into the branch (was 30 behind). `/events` still runs 174 queries (116 cached): not flagged by Bullet, for the perf audit.
- Less vertical space before the intro, events and news boxes at the biggest size (1500px+); fine on the iPad (1366). Scaled the illustration bands from 1920 to 1600px at that tier and moved the boxes up with them: intro margin 1000 to 815px (art 1088px tall, top -915, logo -625), events 220 to 180px, news 220/230 to 183/192px. Intro panel top moved from 1082 to 897px at 1512 wide. Live keeps the Elm values; this is a deliberate departure.
- Event card title too big on the iPhone: live steps the title (1.2rem, then 1.5rem from 600px) and the day number (2.5rem, then 3.12rem with a -0.75rem nudge from 600px); dev had the desktop sizes at every width. Applied live's steps.
- Too much space under the last two cards before "View all events": live 81px, dev 153px (design's 96px box padding plus a 20px list margin). Box bottom padding back to 48px, list margin 0; the design's 32px side padding kept.
- Phone: a bit less space above "View all events". Live and dev both had 4rem box padding below 600px; set the events box to 3rem at every width (live only does that from 600px up), so the gap under the last card is 81px on phones too. Deliberate departure from live.
- Less space below "View all news": news section margin-bottom 240 to 200px on phones and 175 to 150px from 600px, with the lower illustration band moved up by the same amount. Deliberate departure from live.
- Less vertical space between event cards, still too much after the first pass because core's card adds a 2rem bottom margin on top of the theme's top margin. Card bottom margin now 0; gap is 2rem on phones and 2.5rem from 600px up (was 5rem/5.5rem in effect). Deliberate departure from live and the design.
- Latest news box: more top and bottom padding. First pass matched the events box (28/48px) and still looked tight; live's news box is 1rem/4rem, 2rem/4rem from 600px and 3rem/4rem from 1200px (measured 48/64px at 1512), so matched live. Design's 32px sides kept. Image: Kim saw it clearly narrower on dev in Safari (iPad); my Chrome measurements said 303px on both and were wrong for Safari. Safari shrinks the flex-item image to make room for the title column, and the narrower dev card squeezes it harder. Fixed with `flex-shrink: 0` on the image and `min-width: 0` on the text column. Lesson: measure in the browser the reviewer is using. Confirmed against the design source afterwards: `NewsCard.jsx` sets `flex: 'none'` and `aspectRatio: '303 / 202'` on the image; the port dropped both (and used `height: 56.25%`). Both restored. The handoff zip is `~/Downloads/The Trans Dimension Design System.zip` (8 Sep); unpacked to `design/` in the theme repo (untracked) so the review can check against it.
- Note for the rest of the review: the type-size preference is general ("in general I prefer the font sizes on the live site"), so check every page's body and card text against live, not just home.
